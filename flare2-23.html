CONTROLS

DESKTOP · MOUSE & KEYBOARD
MOUSE  moves = look (click the game once to lock it) · ESC releases the mouse & pauses
LEFT BUTTON  DASH (smashes shades & crates)
RIGHT BUTTON  CAST fireball
W A S D / ARROWS  walk forward, back, strafe left / right
SHIFT  run faster · J / L  turn · I / K  look up / down (INVERT LOOK in OPTIONS flips mouse, stick, keys & touch)
H  HEAL · F  throw FLARE
1 Heal · 2 Flare · 3 True Sight · 4 Wrath · 5 Judgement · 6 Create
SPACE  DASH too · F1 or ?  this help
GAMEPAD · PC
LEFT STICK  walk & strafe · RIGHT STICK  look
A or LEFT TRIGGER / BUMPER  DASH
B or RIGHT TRIGGER / BUMPER  CAST
X (HOLD)  spell wheel stays open · push LEFT STICK to pick · release X to cast
Y  HEAL · LEFT STICK CLICK  throw FLARE · START  pause
BUTTONS WRONG? OPTIONS → GAMEPAD SETUP, then press the button you want for each action.
MENUS: D-PAD or LEFT STICK highlights · A select · B back · left / right changes a highlighted slider
PHONE · TOUCH
Drag the LEFT side to move · drag the RIGHT side to look · tap the round buttons for DASH, CAST, HEAL and hold POWERS for the wheel · CUSTOMIZE CONTROLS (pause menu) moves the buttons.
GOAL
Rescue the souls, avoid or judge the golems, light the way and reach the black exit. Mouse sensitivity is in OPTIONS.
